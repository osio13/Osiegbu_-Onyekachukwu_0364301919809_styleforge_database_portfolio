# AI Use Declaration

OpenAI Codex was used as a supporting development tool during this portfolio. It helped produce starting points for the repository structure, SQL and MongoDB examples, test ideas, and documentation. It was also used to assist with local testing, debugging, evidence organisation, and improving the clarity of the written material.

I remained responsible for the submitted work. I reviewed and revised the database designs, scripts, queries, diagrams, explanations, and evidence; checked the recorded results against the executed tests; reviewed the cited technical sources; and made the final decisions about what to include. I understand the implementation and can explain the design choices, transaction and locking behaviour, query-plan findings, MongoDB model, and distributed-database proposal.

The cloud architecture is a design proposal rather than a completed cloud deployment. The practical evidence is limited to the locally verified PostgreSQL and MongoDB exercises described in the evidence register. AI assistance was used to support development and review, not as a substitute for evidence or personal understanding.
